# OpenTTD: Stik Edition

OpenTTD is a business simulation game where you run a transport company. You build railways, roads, airports, and shipping lanes, then move passengers and cargo between towns and industries to earn money. Towns grow, industries produce goods, and rival companies compete with you for the best routes.

Stik Edition is a fork of OpenTTD that adds city building, deep company finance, and venture capital, inspired by Cities: Skylines and Coffee Inc 2.

## What's new compared to regular OpenTTD

### City Building
- Build towns yourself! Place residential and commercial buildings with the new **City Building toolbar** (found in the Town menu)
- Every town has live **demand bars** for Residential, Commercial, and Industrial zones (open a town window and hit the **Zones** button)
- Buildings only go up where there's demand: residents want jobs, shops want customers, industries want workers
- A **Fund Industry** button lets you cover industrial demand directly
- Growing a town yourself means more passengers and mail for your own routes

### Company Finance
- Your company has a real **market valuation**, based on how profitable you actually are (shown in the finances window)
- **Take your company public!** File an IPO once you're established: 3+ years old, 4 straight profitable quarters, and a £500k+ valuation. Going public pays you 25% of your valuation in cash
- Watch your **share price** rise and fall with your profits every quarter
- Pay **dividends** to your shareholders: one-off payouts, or set an automatic policy (up to 100% of quarterly profit)

### Venture Capital
- Invest in **12 fictional startups** like Otter Orbital, Lunar Laundry, and Saturn Sandwich Co.
- Watch them grow through Seed, Series A, B, and C funding rounds
- High risk, high reward: most startups go bankrupt and wipe your stake, but survivors pay out **3x on IPO** or a **30% premium** when acquired
- Open the Ventures window from your company finances

### A Real AI Opponent
- **AAAHogEx** comes bundled, a serious competitor that builds trains, trucks, ships, and planes
- It plays with the new features too: it goes public, pays dividends, invests in startups, and even builds houses near its own stations to grow its traffic

Your existing OpenTTD savegames load right in. Have fun!

# OpenTTD: Stik Edition

## What is OpenTTD?

OpenTTD is a business simulation game where you run a transport company, starting in 1950 with nothing but a bank loan. You build the infrastructure yourself: lay railway track, signals, and stations; pave roads and bus stops; construct airports, docks, and canals. Then you buy vehicles, set up routes, and earn money moving passengers and cargo where they need to go.

The world is alive around you. Towns grow on their own and demand services. Coal mines, farms, forests, factories, and dozens of other industries produce cargo that wants transporting, and many feed into each other: coal goes to power stations, livestock and grain to factories, goods from factories back to towns. Finding profitable chains and building efficient networks around them is the heart of the game.

Time passes, and the game evolves with it. New engines, trucks, planes, and ships are introduced over the decades, from steam locomotives to maglev trains. Vehicles age and break down, towns judge you by how well you serve them, and rival companies (human or AI) race you to the best routes. A game can span more than a century, and a well-run network becomes a sprawling, satisfying machine.

It's free, open source, and endlessly moddable, with thousands of community-made vehicle sets, industries, and maps.

## What is Stik Edition?

Stik Edition is a fork of OpenTTD that adds city building, deep company finance, and venture capital, inspired by Cities: Skylines and Coffee Inc 2. In regular OpenTTD you can only watch towns grow and only ever finance your company with bank loans. Stik Edition puts both in your hands.

### City Building
- Build towns yourself! Place residential and commercial buildings with the new **City Building toolbar** (found in the Town menu)
- Every town has live **demand bars** for Residential, Commercial, and Industrial zones (open a town window and hit the **Zones** button)
- Buildings only go up where there's demand: residents want jobs, shops want customers, industries want workers
- A **Fund Industry** button lets you cover industrial demand directly
- Growing a town yourself means more passengers and mail for your own routes

### Company Finance
- Your company has a real **market valuation**, based on how profitable you actually are (shown in the finances window)
- **Take your company public!** File an IPO once you're established: 3+ years old, 4 straight profitable quarters, and a £500k+ valuation. Going public pays you 25% of your valuation in cash
- Watch your **share price** rise and fall with your profits every quarter
- Pay **dividends** to your shareholders: one-off payouts, or set an automatic policy (up to 100% of quarterly profit)

### Venture Capital
- Invest in **12 fictional startups** like Otter Orbital, Lunar Laundry, and Saturn Sandwich Co.
- Watch them grow through Seed, Series A, B, and C funding rounds
- High risk, high reward: most startups go bankrupt and wipe your stake, but survivors pay out **3x on IPO** or a **30% premium** when acquired
- Open the Ventures window from your company finances

### A Real AI Opponent
- **AAAHogEx** comes bundled, a serious competitor that builds trains, trucks, ships, and planes
- It plays with the new features too: it goes public, pays dividends, invests in startups, and even builds houses near its own stations to grow its traffic

Your existing OpenTTD savegames load right in. Have fun!
